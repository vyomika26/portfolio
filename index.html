About me

I'm Vyomika, a product designer based in San Francisco with roots in Mumbai. Over 8 years I've shaped experiences for Fortune 500s and scrappy startups — from Tata Motors, ZS Associates, and Unilever to 0→1 AI products.

I hold a Bachelor's in Visual Design from Srishti Institute and a Master's in Interaction Design from CCA. I'm a design systems nerd, a mentor on ADPList (2,000+ minutes), and co-founder of Reeb Inc., where I made a board game that sold on Amazon.

Outside work: biking around SF, teaching kids to draw fearlessly, and plotting my 9th country.
