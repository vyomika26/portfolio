01 — Overview

What is Habit Coach AI and why does it matter?

Habit Coach AI is a 24/7 AI-powered accountability companion that helps users build habits through proactive phone calls, conversational tracking, and personalized coaching. Think of it as a personal coach in your pocket: one that actually reaches out to you.

When I joined as the first designer, the product had early functionality but no design foundation. The experience was inconsistent, the brand had no identity, and trust signals were missing entirely.

My role
First and only designer. I defined the interaction model, built the design system from zero, redesigned onboarding, created the habit tracker, chat UI, voice experience, and all marketing assets.
02 — Problem

Users dropped off before they ever saw the product

The core onboarding asked users to enter a phone number and continue via SMS, with no context, no trust signals, and no explanation of what the product actually does. For a habit-building app, that's a fatal first impression.

Beyond onboarding, there was no visual habit tracker (data lived in Google Sheets), no design system, and inconsistent UI across every touchpoint. 95% of users dropped off before experiencing anything.

04 — Research & Insights

Three patterns that shaped every decision

I combined Amplitude drop-off data, user feedback sessions, and competitor analysis to identify what was failing and why. Three themes emerged consistently.

Motivation
Users needed visible progress and clear benefits to stay engaged. Apps like Duolingo use streaks and Headspace uses session counts. Both make effort feel rewarding before asking for commitment.
Trust
Asking for a phone number with zero context is a trust killer. Calm and Notion AI delay personal data collection until after the user has experienced something meaningful.
Discoverability
Entry points buried in menus kill participation. Amazon surfaces review prompts immediately after purchase. We had no surfacing strategy at all.

The phone number ask came cold. Users had no context for the product or why it needed their number. No trust had been built yet.
The tradeoff
Longer flow, higher trust
Moving the number ask to the end meant more steps. But users who got there were already invested. Completion rate mattered more than funnel length.
What changed
Coach selection first
Opening with coach selection made the experience feel personal from tap one. The number ask at the end felt earned, not intrusive.

07 — Design System

A design system built from zero, used in production from day one

I built the full Habit Coach AI design system in Figma while the product was actively being developed. Tokens first, components second, patterns third. Engineers pulled directly from the library.

Dark mode as default
Built simultaneously with light mode, treated as the primary experience for evening users.
Indigo as the brand anchor
Used sparingly as the primary action color. Trustworthy and calm, not clinical or cold.
Personality through emoji
An emoji picker on habit cards: small call, high-satisfaction. Personalization builds ownership.

10 — Learnings

What I'd tell the designer who walked into that first day

⚡
Ship smaller, measure faster
Tight 1-week cycles beat big redesigns every time. Data from week 1 told me exactly what week 2 needed to fix.
🤝
Trust is a design problem, not a copy problem
The real fix was structural: delay the risky ask, show value first. That's a layout decision, not a word decision.
🧱
A design system is worth the upfront investment
By month 3, engineers were shipping new screens with zero back-and-forth. The time saved in reviews was worth 3x the build time.
🧭
Being first means deciding what "done" looks like
No design org, no review process, no historical reference. I had to build the judgment calls and the process to evaluate them at the same time.
