ZS ZAIDYN — Incentive dashboards for pharma sales teams

Redesigned multi-platform sales dashboards for pharmaceutical field teams — transforming fragmented data into a unified, insight-driven experience used by thousands of reps.

The problem

Pharma sales teams were drowning in fragmented data

Pharmaceutical and life sciences companies relied on outdated systems that spread critical data across multiple disconnected tools. Field reps, managers, and executives had no single source of truth for performance, incentives, or goals.

The result: high operational costs, delayed decisions, and field teams disengaged from the very dashboards meant to guide them.

"How might real-time insights and personalized incentives boost operational effectiveness for field teams?"

Friction points discovered

Four problems hiding in plain sight

01
Poor visual hierarchy
Critical elements like the "Welcome Report" lacked prominence. Reps couldn't tell what mattered at a glance — everything competed for attention equally.
02
Information overload
The "Resources" and "Report Links" sections felt overcrowded. Too much upfront, too early — discouraging interaction before reps even got started.
03
Unclear priorities
Competing elements diluted focus. Without a clear hierarchy of what to act on first, field reps defaulted to workarounds and manual processes.
04
User disengagement
Irrelevant upfront content discouraged interaction. The dashboard felt like a chore, not a tool. Reps skipped it entirely and lost trust in the data.
Design solutions

Six components, one unified experience

01
Slider Banner
Clickable hero banner directing to detailed event pages. Prominent allocation for text and imagery — immediately communicating what's featured and why it matters.
02
Insights Section
Hierarchical structure using subject → heading → date. Removed visual clutter (graphs, images) for pure information clarity. Individual clickable cards with a "View All" escape hatch.
03
Annual Awards
Enhanced visibility in performance rankings with design system icons. Clear profit/loss indicators with directional arrows, and updated timeline representation for at-a-glance reading.
04
QTD Total Payout
Improved clarity on financial metrics with visual gain/loss indicators. Marginal up/down arrows giving reps immediate context on where they stand in the quarter.
05
Quick Links
Responsive icon layout with background organization. Enlarged, center-aligned icons — each individually clickable — removing navigational friction from common workflows.
06
Upcoming Events
Structured layout: date → month → event name → organizer. Integrated action buttons with logo color coordination for quick visual grouping across management events.
