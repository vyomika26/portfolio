Multi-Platform Incentives Dashboard
The Challenge
  • Data on performance, incentives, and goals was spread across multiple tools, making access and action difficult for sales teams, managers, and executive leadership within life sciences and pharmaceutical companies
  • Without a centralized view, tracking progress and payouts was time-consuming, leading to delays and errors in decision-making. This hindered team efficiency, organizational performance, and strategic alignment, particularly for professionals striving to optimize operations and drive data-informed decisions

How might we provide field teams real-time insights and personalized incentives to boost operational effectiveness?

Design Process
Discovery Collaborated with Product Managers (PMs) and the Head of UX to gather design requirements and insights, ensuring a clear understanding and user expectations.
Analysis Conducted a thorough walk-through of pre-developed screens to analyze current workflows, identify areas for improvement, and align designs with product objectives and user-centric principles.
Mapping Friction Points Identified key usability gaps in the existing dashboard experience that created barriers for field reps.
Fundamental Friction Points
Key elements, like the "Welcome Report," occupy prominent areas but fail to stand out visually, reducing their impact.
Sections like "Resources" and "Report Links" are overcrowded, making navigation overwhelming and discouraging interaction.
The design lacks a clear focus, with too many elements competing for attention, making it difficult to identify key information.
Users will likely tune out notifications that show irrelevant presented options, as it lacks personalization relative to their immediate goals.
Design explorations
Incorporating peer feedback during iterations to craft the final polished wireframes
