ZS Persona — Design system for user-centric product development

Built a scalable persona design system that enabled 80+ personas to be generated in a single day — empowering designers and researchers across ZS to make decisions grounded in real users.

Role
Visual Designer
Timeline
3 months · March 2023
Type
Design System · Documentation
Industry
Healthcare Tech
The problem

A complex product ecosystem with no shared language for users

The ZAIDYN product suite served a vast, complex user ecosystem across pharma sales. But every team had a different definition of who their users were — leading to fragmented product decisions and empathy gaps that grew with every new feature shipped.

30+
Distinct user personas needed across a single product platform — with no unified format to represent them.
Multi
Team — researchers, designers, and engineers each had their own definition of the same user.
80+
Personas ultimately generated in a single day — once the system was ready for company-wide use.
"How might we deepen empathy in our design process to enable seamless integration and customization as we expand to new user groups?"
— Central design question, ZS Persona System
Design principles

Four principles that shaped every component

Empathy-Driven
Design with deep user understanding and emotional connection — personas should feel like real people, not data summaries.
Scalable & Flexible
Adaptable templates that work for 2 personas or 200 — scaling to new user groups without breaking down.
Data-Informed
Let research data guide structure while balancing creative storytelling — grounded in facts, readable at a glance.
Inclusive & Simple
Prioritize accessibility and simplicity — non-Figma users should be able to edit layouts without special expertise.
Design evolution

Three versions. One right answer.

The system evolved through structured feedback sessions with researchers and visual designers — each round surfacing something the previous version got wrong.

v1.0
Foundation
Basic information hierarchy
Single quote per persona
No org chart integration
Separate skills/tools sections
v1.5
Refined
Scaled down visuals for 30+ personas
Text-focused with pull-quotes
Editable by non-Figma users
Variable content levels
v2.0 · Final
Shipped
Persona group tags on right side
Org chart in "Day in Life" section
Standardized Name, Title labels
Reduced text to 20px for scale
What shipped

A master file that anyone could open and use

→
Master file template
A single Figma source of truth with fully editable components — optimized for 1920×1080 so it worked across web and presentation formats without reformatting.
→
Comprehensive usage guide
Detailed annotations and content guidelines — written so a new ZSer or someone unfamiliar with pharma could understand the personas without needing a briefing.
→
Day in Life org charts
Embedded relationship maps within each persona showing how users connect to each other — giving teams immediate context on collaboration patterns and decision flows.
→
Persona group tags
Right-side tagging system for quick visual identification across 30+ personas — enabling teams to sort, filter, and navigate the full persona library at a glance.
Impact

80 personas in a single day

The system eliminated the bottleneck of one-at-a-time persona creation — enabling the entire design and research organization to collaborate on a shared library at unprecedented speed.
