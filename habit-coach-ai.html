The problem

Habit apps fail because they treat humans like machines

Most habit-building apps are rigid checklists — designed around streaks and gamification, not real human behaviour. When life gets in the way, users fall off and never return.

Three critical gaps existed in every existing solution on the market:

95%
Boost in goal completion when an accountability partner is involved — yet most apps don't have one.
65%
Of users abandon habit apps within a month due to rigid flows that don't adapt to real life.
45%
Drop in engagement without a personalized, coach-like interaction — generic feels hollow.
"I tried every productivity tool, but nothing stuck. Daily calls with a real person worked — but it wasn't scalable. When GPT-3 launched, I saw AI could be that human-like coach."
— Andriy Rusyn, CEO, Habit Coach AI
Design approach

Four principles that guided every decision

01
Progressive disclosure
Surface information contextually and in the right moment — reducing cognitive load and avoiding overwhelming new users from the start.
02
Human connection
Warm, supportive feedback that mimics real coaching relationships. The AI should feel like a person who genuinely cares — not a bot running a script.
03
Guided flow
Clear, unambiguous navigation that removes friction at every step. No dead ends, no confusion, no second-guessing.
04
Multi-modal accessibility
Designed for voice and text interactions — users can call or text their AI coach anytime. The experience adapts to however someone wants to show up.
What we built

From SMS onboarding to daily AI check-ins

The product started with a single insight: the lowest friction way to start is not a form — it's a phone number. Users enter their number; the AI coach follows up via SMS with progressive questions, gathering context for full personalization without any downloads or sign-up flows.

01
SMS-first onboarding
No app download required. Phone number entry → AI coach textes within seconds → progressive questions build a full user profile over the first few days.
02
Daily AI check-ins
Consistent daily touchpoints with empathetic, personalized messages. The AI remembers what you said yesterday — and the day before. It adapts tone based on how you're doing.
03
Voice + text access
Users can call or text their coach anytime. The interface seamlessly transitions between modalities — from a 5-second voice note to a full journaling session.
04
Sketch-test-repeat loop
Monitored Amplitude analytics daily to find drop-off points. Each finding fed directly back into Figma — design guidelines updated in real time, not in sprint retrospectives.
