Teaching
Drawing, painting, and building things with kids

I've taught creative drawing, painting, and hands-on craft and construction to kids ages
3 to 13 at a summer camp. Most of it is less about the finished piece and more about getting
a 5-year-old to commit to a shape before they second-guess it.

I'm a trained designer, a B.Des in Visual Communication and an M.Des from California College of the Arts, and I'm currently studying Early Childhood Education. Teaching kids and designing products turned out to ask the same question: how do you make something approachable enough that someone just starts.
