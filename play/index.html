Teaching
Drawing, painting, and building things with kids

Every summer, I end up on the floor with paper, paint, and kids ages 3 to 13. Most of it isn't about the finished piece. It's about getting a 5-year-old to commit to a shape before they second-guess it.

I came to this sideways: a B.Des in Visual Communication, an M.Des from California College of the Arts, and now I'm studying Early Childhood Education, because teaching kids and designing products kept asking me the same question. How do you make something approachable enough that someone just starts.
